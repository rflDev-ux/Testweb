A table worth the evening

Maison Aurelle began as a single room with six tables and a conviction: that great cooking needs time, good produce and a quiet place to be enjoyed.

Today the menu follows the market. Fish arrives from Brittany and Cornwall, vegetables from two farms within an hour of the kitchen, and every sauce is built from stock we simmer for days.
